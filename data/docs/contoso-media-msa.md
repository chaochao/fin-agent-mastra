# Master Services Agreement — Contoso Media

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Company
("Provider") and Contoso Media ("Customer").

## 1. Payment terms
Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice
date (net-30). Invoices are issued monthly in arrears. Payments are made in USD via
ACH or wire transfer.

## 2. Late payments
Any amount not paid within the net-30 window accrues interest at one and one-half
percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
Provider may suspend services if an invoice is more than sixty (60) days overdue.

## 3. Term and renewal
The initial term is twelve (12) months from the effective date. The Agreement renews
automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives at least
sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal before the end of the current term.

## 4. Service levels
Provider targets 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below 99.0% in a calendar
month, Customer is entitled to a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of that
month's fees, applied to the next invoice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with at least the same
care it uses for its own, and shall not disclose it to third parties without prior
written consent, except as required by law.

## 6. Limitation of liability
Except for breaches of confidentiality, each party's aggregate liability is limited to
the total fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
