# Travel & Expense Policy

This policy governs reimbursable business expenses for all employees and contractors.

## 1. General principles
Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred for a legitimate business purpose.
Submit expenses within thirty (30) days of the transaction with an itemized receipt.
Any single expense over five hundred dollars ($500) requires manager pre-approval.

## 2. Software subscriptions
New recurring software subscriptions must be approved by a team lead before purchase.
Subscriptions over one hundred dollars ($100) per month require finance sign-off.
Unused seats should be cancelled promptly; refunds are credited back to the card on file.

## 3. Travel
Book economy class for flights under six (6) hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to three
hundred dollars ($300) per night in standard cities. Ride-share and taxis are
reimbursable; personal-car mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate.

## 4. Meals and entertainment
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to seventy-five dollars ($75) per day.
Client entertainment requires the names of attendees and the business purpose on the
receipt. Alcohol is not reimbursable except for approved client events.

## 5. Non-reimbursable expenses
The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, traffic fines, late fees on
personal cards, first-class flight upgrades, and gifts over fifty dollars ($50).

## 6. Corporate cards
Corporate card holders must reconcile all charges monthly. Missing receipts over
twenty-five dollars ($25) may be treated as taxable income to the cardholder.
